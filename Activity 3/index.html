ABOUT CLASH OF CLANS

THE WORLD OF CLASH

Clash of Clans is a strategy game where players build and upgrade their villages, train powerful armies, defend their bases, and battle against other players.

Players can use different types of troops, heroes, spells, siege machines, pets, and hero equipment to create powerful attack strategies and overcome enemy defenses.

The game combines village building, resource management, troop upgrades, clan teamwork, and real-time strategic battles. Each unit has unique abilities, strengths, and weaknesses, making strategy an important part of every attack.

This Clash Troops database provides an easy way to explore the different characters and units available in Clash of Clans, including their descriptions, statistics, levels, and upgrades.
