About Clash of Clans

Build a village, train an army, and join a clan to battle players from all over the world.

What is Clash of Clans?

Clash of Clans is a free-to-play mobile strategy game made by Supercell, a game studio from Finland. It was released in 2012 and quickly became one of the most popular mobile games in the world.

You are the chief of your own village. You collect resources, construct buildings and defenses, train troops, and attack other players' villages to steal their loot, all while protecting your own from raids.

How the game works

1 · Build
Your village
Place collectors, storages, defenses and walls. Upgrading your Town Hall unlocks stronger buildings and troops.
2 · Train
Your army
Spend Elixir to train troops such as Barbarians, Archers and Dragons, and mix them into a strategy that fits your target.
3 · Battle
Raid and defend
Attack other villages for loot and trophies, while your defenses protect your own resources when you are away.
4 · Team up
Join a clan
Chat and donate troops with clanmates, and fight together in Clan Wars against other clans.

Resources

Gold
Used to build and upgrade most buildings, defenses and walls.
Elixir
Used to train troops and upgrade them. Every troop on this site is trained with Elixir.
Dark Elixir
A rarer resource for training powerful dark troops and upgrading Heroes.

Understanding a troop

Favorite target
What a troop goes after first: Any, Resources, Defenses or Walls. On this site it decides the card color.
Attack type
How it fights: Melee, Ranged, Area Splash, Healing or Chain Lightning.
Ground and Air
Whether the troop can hit ground units only, or both ground and air.
Training cost
The resource spent to train the troop, such as Elixir.
